## Core Frameworks & Mental Models
- **Minimax Theorem**: In finite, two-person zero-sum games, every game has a rational solution (a value) achievable through mixed strategies.
- **Nash Equilibrium**: A set of strategies where no player has an incentive to unilaterally deviate. Use when analyzing non-cooperative games.
- **The Core**: The set of stable allocations in an n-person game where no coalition can improve its payoff by breaking away.
- **Shapley Value**: A method to fairly distribute the total gains of cooperation among players according to their marginal contributions.
