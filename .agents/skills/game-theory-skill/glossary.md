**Banzhaf Power Index** — A measure of political power based on the number of critical swings a voter has in all possible winning coalitions.
**Core** — The set of undominated imputations; outcomes where no coalition can do better by breaking away.
**Minimax** — The strategy of minimizing the maximum possible loss.
**Nash Equilibrium** — A profile of strategies where no player can unilaterally deviate and increase their payoff.
**Pure Strategy** — A deterministic choice of action.
**Saddle Point** — An outcome in a zero-sum game that is both the minimum of its row and the maximum of its column.
**Shapley Value** — An allocation of payoffs that reflects each player's average marginal contribution.
