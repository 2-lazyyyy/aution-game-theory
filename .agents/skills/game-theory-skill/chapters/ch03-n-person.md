# Chapter 3: N-Person Games

## Core Idea
In games with three or more players, the focus shifts from individual strategies to coalition formation and the fair distribution of coalition payoffs.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Core**:
  - When to use: To determine if a proposed payoff allocation is stable against defection by any subgroup.
- **The Shapley Value**:
  - When to use: To distribute the total payoff fairly based on each player's marginal contribution to all possible coalitions.
- **Power Indices (Banzhaf / Shapley-Shubik)**:
  - When to use: To measure actual voting power in committees or parliaments, rather than just vote share.

## Key Takeaways
1. A player's power in an n-person game depends on how crucial they are to forming winning coalitions.
2. The Core can be empty (no stable outcome) or very large; the Shapley Value always provides a unique 'fair' allocation.
